1. Summary

The main board is built for receive. To transmit you must fit the final transistor, the transformers, the low-pass filters, the SWR bridge and the six relays, and remove the factory receive jumper. The relays are driven by the firmware through the PCA9555 I²C expander.

TX chain: RF from the FST3253 (TXSIG) → Q5 µPC1678G (pre-amp) → Q6 RD01MUS1 (driver) → T4 → Q4 RD16HHF1 (final) → T3 → RELAY-6 (TX/RX) → band LPF (RELAY-1…5) → T1/T2 bridge → antenna.

On receive, RELAY-6 routes the antenna (already filtered by the band LPF) to the mixer input (RXFST → D1 → FST3253).

⚠️ Receive jumper. From the factory a red wire connects the antenna connector (BNC) straight to the receiver input (D1), bypassing relays and filters. Remove it when fitting RELAY-6 and the LPF relays. Transmitting with it in place feeds the power into the receive mixer and destroys the FST3253.

Consequence for receive: while that jumper is there the receiver has no RF filtering at all in front of the mixer.

3. Before you start

Recommended tools and equipment

  • Soldering station (hot air and fine tip), flux and loupe/microscope.
  • Multimeter, continuity tester and, if possible, oscilloscope and spectrum analyser.
  • Dummy load, power/SWR meter and a power supply with adjustable current limit.
  • ESD wrist strap and mat.

Preliminary checks

  • Identify your board revision. This guide is based on Rev. 2.0 (MAIN UNIT v2.0).
  • Check which parts your unit actually has against the table in §2.
  • Make sure the supply delivers 13.8 V and at least 2 A before connecting the power stage.

4. Parts to add

The shopping list, with quantities and spares, is in Lista-compra-TX.md (Spanish).

4.1. Semiconductors

Ref. Part Qty Function
Q4 RD16HHF1 (TO-220) 1 Power final (~5 W). The tab is the source (ground): no insulator needed.

Q5 (µPC1678G), Q6 (RD01MUS1), Q2 (SI2301), Q3 (2N7002) and diodes D3/D4 (1N5711WS) are already fitted. The production BOM lists PT9926-SOP8 for Q5: that is a BOM error (a dual MOSFET); the fitted part is the µPC1678G in the schematic.

4.2. Relays

Ref. Part Qty Function
RELAY-1…5 Hongfa HFD4/5-SR (SMD, DPDT, 5 V coil) 5 LPF selection
RELAY-6 Hongfa HFD4/5-SR 1 TX/RX switching

The schematic calls them G6K-2Y-Y, but the board footprint is RELAY-SMD_HFD4/5-SR and the production BOM gives HFD4/5-S (LCSC C64399). An Omron G6K-2F-Y DC5 is electrically equivalent (5 V coil, ~21 mA); check that it fits the footprint before buying it.

The coils are driven directly from the PCA9555 outputs (~21 mA each). Check that every relay switches firmly when changing band.

5. Low-pass filters (LPF)

Each filter is a 7-element low-pass (4 shunt capacitors and 3 inductors). The band of each one is derived from its cut-off frequency, calculated from the schematic values (T37-2: AL ≈ 4.0 nH/turn²; T37-6: AL ≈ 3.0 nH/turn²):

Relay PCA9555 line Band Core and turns Inductance Capacitors Approx. cut-off
RELAY-1 BAND1 160 m T37-2: L1 32, L2 37, L3 32 4.1 / 5.5 / 4.1 µH C47 820p, C48 2.2n, C49 2.2n, C50 820p 2.9 MHz
RELAY-3 BAND2 80 m T37-2: L7 22, L8 27, L9 22 1.9 / 2.9 / 1.9 µH C55 470p, C56 1.2n, C57 1.2n, C58 470p 5.4 MHz
RELAY-5 BAND3 60–40 m T37-6: L13 21, L14 24, L15 21 1.3 / 1.7 / 1.3 µH C67 270p, C68 680p, C69 680p, C70 270p 9.3 MHz
RELAY-2 BAND4 30–20 m T37-6: L4 16, L5 17, L6 16 0.77 / 0.87 / 0.77 µH C51 180p, C52 390p, C53 390p, C54 180p 17 MHz
RELAY-4 BAND5 17–10 m T37-6: L10 10, L11 11, L12 10 0.30 / 0.36 / 0.30 µH C59 120p, C60 220p, C61 220p, C62 120p 36 MHz

Relay numbering does not follow band order. The X1 Custom firmware already uses this mapping (LPF1 = 160 m, LPF2 = 80 m, LPF3 = 40 m, LPF4 = 30–20 m, LPF5 = 17–10 m).

Correction to earlier versions of this guide, which shifted the bands by one position (80/40/30/20/10 m).

Winding: 0.4 mm enamelled wire (AWG 26), turns spread around the whole toroid. One "turn" is each pass of the wire through the hole. If you have an analyser (NanoVNA), measure each filter: insertion loss < 0.5 dB in band and 2nd-harmonic rejection > 40 dB. Adjust by spreading or compressing turns.

6. Power stage and transformers

  • Q5 → Q6 → Q4 form the amplifier chain. Q4 is the final (RD16HHF1, ~5 W) and Q6 its driver (RD01MUS1).
  • Q4 is screwed (M3) onto its thermal pad against the chassis/heatsink, with thermal paste. It dissipates several watts: never transmit without it properly fixed.
  • T3 and T4 are bifilar transformers on FT37-43 (footprint FT37-43 BIF, 4 pins; the board sets the connections). The schematic gives no turns. Starting values (estimated, not verified): 8–10 bifilar turns of 0.3–0.4 mm wire on each. With the 1:4 connection the schematic suggests, Q4's drain sees ~12.5 Ω, consistent with about 5 W at 13.8 V.

Keep RF leads short with a nearby ground return.

6.1. Bias

  • Q6 bias is set by R49 and Q4 bias by R50, both fed from the switched +9 V (present only on TX).
  • Before the first TX power-up, turn R50 (and R49) to minimum gate voltage.
  • Into a dummy load, with the supply limited to 1.5 A and no drive, key TX and raise R50 slowly to a Q4 idle current of about 100–200 mA above the RX current. Do the same with R49 for Q6 (about 20–50 mA).
  • Gate voltage should stay below about 4 V (RD16HHF1, typical Vth 2–3 V). If the current runs away or Q4 heats at idle, cut power and check the assembly.

Reference: the MBPTRX procedure for its 2× RD16HHF1 stage (https://github.com/ianm8/MBPTRX/blob/main/PCB/README.md), to be scaled to a single final. Not validated on the X1.

7. SWR bridge

T1 and T2 form a tandem coupler. The parallel load resistors (R41∥R42 and R43∥R44, 2 × 100 Ω = 50 Ω) point to the classic design:

Transformer Primary Secondary
T1 1 pass (the antenna conductor goes through the toroid) 10 turns
T2 1 pass 10 turns

Coupling ≈ −20 dB. The detectors are D3/D4 (1N5711WS) and the reading is digitised by the ADS1115 (FWD on AIN0, REF on AIN1). Calibrate with a 50 Ω load and a known mismatched load.

Earlier versions of this guide proposed 1.5/10 and 4/30 turns; they are replaced by the symmetric tandem, consistent with the board's load resistors.

8. TX stage supply

According to the schematic (sheet 3):

  1. U11 (78M09) makes +9 V from +13.8 V. It also feeds the LM386 audio amplifier, so it is always fitted.
  2. TXENABLE (PCA9555) turns on Q3 (2N7002), which opens Q2 (SI2301).
  3. Q2 delivers the switched +9 V to Q6's drain (through T4) and to the bias trimmers R49/R50. On RX there is no voltage: the transistors are cut off.
  4. Q4's drain gets +13.8 V through T3 permanently (without bias it does not conduct).
  5. Q5 (µPC1678G, already fitted) is fed from the +5V_B net (Vcc and output, through FB4). That net does not appear on any other schematic sheet.

⚠️ Before the first TX, find out with a meter where +5V_B comes from. The µPC1678G takes at most 6 V: it must not get the 9 V. Also check whether +5V_B is present on RX or only on TX.

9. Bring-up and verification

  1. Visual inspection: joints, bridges, orientation of transistors and relays. Red antenna wire removed.
  2. Continuity: no shorts between +13.8 V, +9 V, +5 V and ground.
  3. Power without RF: power up with a low current limit and check +5 V, +9 V, switched +9 V (TX only) and +5V_B (≤ 6 V).
  4. Relays: change band from the firmware and confirm the right filter relay closes (table in §5) and that RELAY-6 switches on TX.
  5. RX: check that receive still works through RELAY-6 and the band filter.
  6. Bias as in §6.1.
  7. TX into a dummy load: low power first and for a few seconds. Watch current, Q4 temperature and SWR.
  8. Tuning: raise power gradually, check harmonics with an analyser if you have one and validate the SWR reading with a mismatched load.
  9. Antenna: only after all of the above.

Never transmit without a load or antenna connected. The X1 Custom firmware keeps TX locked until «Etapa TX instalada» (TX stage fitted) is enabled in the menu, and applies a TX timeout and a high-SWR cut. The firmware TX has not been tested on air yet.

10. Points to verify

  • +5V_B: source and voltage before powering Q5 (max. 6 V). See §8.
  • R49/R50: already fitted. The schematic says 4.7 kΩ, but the BOM part number (3296W-1-501) is 500 Ω; keep it in mind when setting the bias.
  • Q5 in the BOM: PT9926-SOP8 is an error; the correct part is the µPC1678G.
  • Relays: HFD4/5-SR footprint. Check pinout and coil voltage before soldering.
  • T3/T4 and T1/T2 turns: estimated, not measured on this radio.
  • Q4/Q6 bias: needs a procedure and measurement.
  • Dissipation: check Q4 temperature at sustained power.
  • Board revision: this guide assumes Rev. 2.0.
